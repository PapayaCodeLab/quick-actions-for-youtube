Privacy Policy

Quick Actions for YouTube — last updated 5 September 2026

This extension collects nothing, sends nothing and has no server.

What is stored, and where

The following is stored on your own device only, using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API:

  • Which of your playlists you pinned, and the icon or image chosen for each
  • Your settings from the options page
  • A local cache of the video IDs in your pinned playlists, used to highlight playlists that already contain a video
  • Images you upload as a playlist symbol, downscaled to 96 px and stored as WebP

None of it leaves your browser. It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or tracking of any kind
  • No accounts, no login, no advertising, no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell or share
  • No remote code: all code ships inside the extension package
  • No network requests to any server operated by the developer. There is no backend.

Permissions

  • storage — saves your pinned playlists, their symbols and your settings on your device.
  • Access to https://www.youtube.com/* — the extension only runs on youtube.com. It adds its buttons to the YouTube pages you open and reads the contents of your own pinned playlists (the same pages your browser would load if you clicked them) to know which playlists already contain a video. It runs on no other website.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. You can also unpin playlists individually at any time.
