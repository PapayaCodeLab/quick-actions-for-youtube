# Quick Actions for YouTube

Everyday YouTube chores in one click: clear your watch history, save to your playlists, change the speed, grab a frame or a thumbnail.

**Chrome Web Store:** coming soon · [Privacy policy](https://papayacodelab.github.io/quick-actions-for-youtube/privacy.html)

## Features

- **One-click delete** — a trash button on every video in your watch history, Liked videos, Downloads and your playlists. *Watch later* also gets a “remove all” button.
- **Pinned playlists** — pin up to 8 playlists. An icon row under every video adds or removes it with one click, and playlists that already contain the video are highlighted. Give each one an icon: 1,791 icons, any emoji, or your own image.
- **Speed controls** — the current speed plus slower, faster, rewind and forward buttons right in the player. Keyboard shortcuts <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>, and <kbd>Q</kbd> switches to your preferred speed and back.
- **Screenshots** — a camera button saves the current frame as a PNG, without controls or captions, named after the channel and the video.
- **Thumbnail download** — “Download thumbnail” in every video’s menu, in the highest resolution available.
- **Your language** — follows YouTube’s interface language, 48 languages included.

Everything matches YouTube’s own design and works in light and dark mode.

## Privacy

No account, no server, no tracking, no ads. Settings stay in your browser, and nothing is ever sent to the developer. Screenshots and thumbnails go straight to your downloads folder. The extension only runs on youtube.com and contains no remote code. → [Privacy policy](https://papayacodelab.github.io/quick-actions-for-youtube/privacy.html)

## Install

**From the Chrome Web Store:** coming soon.

**From source:**

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder.

Settings: right-click the extension icon → **Options**.
